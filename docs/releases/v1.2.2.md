### PS4: catching up with 1.2.1

- Works on firmware 13.52 and 14.00.
- Finds and syncs more emulators: pSNES, pNES, pGEN, pGBA and pFBN (now with save states), EmuSuNi, DolphinPS4, NPS64, SNESUI, PS4SX2 (memory card backup), Resonance (save states) and RArch.
- GameCube saves are shared with the PS5. N64 saves are shared between NPS64, EmuSuNi and RetroArch.
- When the sync service isn't running, RommPS says why: never started, not in GoldHEN's AutoRun queue, stopped with an error, or its port is taken.
- The PS4 app is now called RommPS, and installs as an update over the previous one.
- The web UI is faster on the PS4.

### Fixed on both consoles

- Large save states (GameCube's run to tens of MB) sync both ways.
- Game lists with a lot of metadata (PS1, PS2) load.
- Turning on many emulators at once no longer stops the sync service.
