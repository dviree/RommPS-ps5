Update RommPS and open it once. It updates everything else.

### New

- RetroArch 2.0 support. Its games live in new folders, named after each system (`content/Nintendo - Game Boy Advance`). New downloads go there, and RommPS offers to move the games already in the old folders. Saves and states stay where they are.
- RetroArch 2.0 plays PS2 with PS5SX2. Its two shared memory cards are backed up to RomM.
- Covers keep their own shape. Square and wide boxes (SNES, N64 and others) are no longer squeezed into portrait cards.

### Fixed

- PS2 BIOS files for RetroArch go to `system/pcsx2/bios`, where its PS2 cores look for them.

### Good to know

- PS2 save states made with LRPS2 don't load in PS5SX2. Save in-game before upgrading RetroArch.
