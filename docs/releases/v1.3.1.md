RomM Sync 1.3.1 for this fork (dviree/RommPS-ps5), on top of RommPS 1.3.0.

### New

- PS5 games: with a USB drive in, the Emulators page offers **PS5 games (USB homebrew)**. Games from RomM's `ps5` platform download straight to `/mnt/usb0/homebrew`.
- PS5 games show their format in the library, the queue and Downloads: "Astro Bot [FPKG]", "[LZ4]", "[exFAT]", "[FFPKG]", "[FFPFS]" or "[Game folder]". A .pkg is told apart by its name: a PS4 title id (CUSA12345) or the PS4 platform makes it "[PS4 PKG]", and "DLC", "Add-On" or "Season Pass" in its name or RomM's tags makes it "[DLC]" ("[PS4 DLC]" or "[PS5 DLC]" when the title id says which). A game you have in two formats shows as two cards, one per format, and its details say which one it is.
- Updates itself on the PS5: once a day it checks this fork's releases, and installs a newer one as soon as no game, sync or download is running. Turn off "Check for updates daily" in Settings to stop it. Only releases signed with this fork's key install.
